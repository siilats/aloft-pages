Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Aloft Aviation Services operates the empty-leg flight website at chartairinc.com. This policy explains how we handle information from visitors, charter inquiries, email alerts, and our internal advertising reporting.

Information we collect and why

  • Charter inquiries. If you email us, open a chat, or message us on WhatsApp, we may receive your name, contact details, trip preferences, and the information you choose to share. We use it to answer questions, check availability, provide quotes, and coordinate trips when requested. The WhatsApp link opens a draft message in WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it.
  • Empty-leg alerts. The signup form requests your departure and destination airports, email address, and an optional WhatsApp number. We use your email to confirm your subscription and send route matches. We keep a record of confirmation, alert deliveries, and unsubscribe status. A matching subscription can also be sent to the Aloft team so we can follow up on the route. Alert delivery is by email.
  • Website activity. The site and its service providers may receive IP addresses, browser and device information, referring pages, campaign identifiers, cookies, and details about pages viewed and buttons clicked. We use this information to operate and protect the site, understand how people find it, and measure ads and quote-link activity.

Cookies and advertising measurement

We use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Google Tag Manager, and Meta Pixel. Tags configured through Google Tag Manager can also send advertising measurement events to platforms such as LinkedIn and Reddit. Those services may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure ad performance and, depending on their settings and policies, personalize advertising.

When you open a quote link, we may send the click location, travel route, aircraft, date window, and an event identifier. For Meta measurement, our Cloudflare relay also forwards IP address, browser user agent, and existing Meta cookie identifiers when available. Quote-click event fields exclude the text you send on WhatsApp and the contact information you enter in the alert form.

You can manage cookies in your browser and manage ad preferences with the relevant platform. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Contact us using the address below if you want to make a privacy request about advertising measurement.

LinkedIn Ads reporting

When an authorized ad account user connects LinkedIn to our reporting app, we use the Advertising API to retrieve campaign and ad identifiers and performance metrics such as impressions and clicks for the authorized Aloft advertising account. The app is intended for internal advertising reporting. Reports may be retrieved again while access remains authorized and stored in a database available to people who need them for that work.

You can withdraw the app’s access in LinkedIn’s permitted services settings or contact us to request disconnection and deletion of data associated with your authorization. See LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy for how LinkedIn handles information on its services.

Service providers and other disclosures

We use providers for website hosting, infrastructure, email delivery, live chat, and communications, including GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Resend, tawk.to, and WhatsApp. Our analytics and advertising providers receive the measurement information described above. Flight availability links may take you to JetInsight or another service. Those services have their own privacy policies. We may share inquiry information with service providers involved in a trip you request, or disclose information when required by law or to address fraud, safety, or security.

Retention and your choices

How long we keep information depends on why it was collected, whether an inquiry or subscription is still active, and any legal, security, or recordkeeping needs. You can unsubscribe from flight alerts using the link in an alert email. Unsubscribing stops future alerts for that subscription. To request access, correction, or deletion, email us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Any rights or exceptions depend on applicable law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@charteraloft.com. We will post updates to this policy on this page.

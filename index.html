Private jet empty legs from Naples, Miami and beyond

An empty leg is a one-way private jet flight available when an aircraft needs to reposition. If your plans match the route and dates, an empty-leg charter can offer a lower price than arranging a dedicated round trip. Routes and departure times depend on the aircraft's schedule and can change.

Explore Aloft's available flights involving Naples Airport (APF), Miami-Opa locka Executive Airport (OPF), Charleston Executive Airport (JZI) and other destinations in the listings above. Filter by Challenger 601 empty legs or Learjet 35A empty legs, then check the dates and passenger capacity for your flight. Availability at any airport varies with the current schedule.

Empty-leg charter questions

How do I get a firm quote for an empty-leg flight?

Select Request a Quote on a flight to open WhatsApp with its route, dates and aircraft, or use the live chat in the corner of this page. Tell a real person your passenger count and preferred timing. Aloft will confirm availability, the total charter price and applicable conditions before you book.

Can I book a Challenger 601 or Learjet 35A empty leg?

Use the aircraft filter to see current Bombardier Challenger 601 and Learjet 35A listings. Each card shows its route, availability window, estimated duration and seats. Aircraft and schedules remain subject to confirmation.

Are empty-leg prices per seat or for the whole aircraft?

Card estimates cover the whole aircraft and are calculated from the listed flight time and starting hourly rate. Request a firm quote to confirm the total price, inclusions and terms with Aloft.

What if no empty leg matches my plans?

Empty legs have limited routing and timing flexibility. Contact Aloft Aviation for private charter options if you need a different airport, departure time or return flight.

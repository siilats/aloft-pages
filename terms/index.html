Empty-leg listings and estimates

An empty leg is a repositioning flight. Its route and timing depend on the aircraft’s schedule and can change or be withdrawn. Listed dates are availability windows. Confirm the exact airports, timing, aircraft, seating capacity, and availability with Aloft before relying on a listing or arranging other travel.

Where shown, a rate or price estimate is for the whole aircraft unless the listing expressly identifies a per-person offer. Aircraft photos and estimates are for reference. Request a current written quote for the final price, included services, taxes, fees, and any conditions.

Quotes and bookings

Opening a quote link or sending an inquiry by email, chat, or WhatsApp starts a conversation. A booking requires confirmation under the applicable written charter agreement. That agreement governs the flight, including payment, itinerary, passenger requirements, changes, cancellation, and refunds.

When Aloft acts as an air charter broker, the authorized direct air carrier identified for the flight provides the air transportation and has operational control. Aloft will provide any operator and brokerage disclosures required for the proposed charter.
